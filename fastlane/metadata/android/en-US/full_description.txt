akiHz is a lightweight, open-source Android app for switching your device's display refresh rate without root access.

It automatically detects the refresh rates supported by your display and lets you switch between values such as 60 Hz, 90 Hz, and 120 Hz. A Quick Settings tile provides one-tap cycling without opening the app.

Features:
* Automatic detection of supported refresh rates
* Quick Settings tile for fast switching
* OEM-specific support for popular Android manufacturers
* Manual OEM override and custom settings profiles
* Light, dark, and true-black AMOLED themes
* Stable and prerelease update channels

akiHz requires Android 11 or newer and Shizuku to be installed, running, and authorized. Shizuku provides the privileged access needed to change Android's protected refresh-rate settings; root is not required.

Device compatibility depends on the settings exposed by each manufacturer. Xiaomi is the primary tested platform, while support for other manufacturers is provided on a best-effort basis.
