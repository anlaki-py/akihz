akiHz switches your display refresh rate from a Quick Settings tile. Root is not needed. The app uses Shizuku for the privileged access Android normally blocks.

The app reads the rates your display reports, so you pick from real values such as 60 Hz, 90 Hz, and 120 Hz. Choose which rates the tile cycles through, tap the tile, and the change applies right away. Long press the tile to open the app.

Features:
* One-tap refresh rate switching from Quick Settings
* Automatic detection of supported refresh rates
* Pick the rates the tile cycles through
* Background service keeps the tile ready after the app closes
* Settings keys for Xiaomi, Samsung, OnePlus, OPPO, vivo, realme, ASUS, Motorola, Sony, and Pixel
* Manual OEM override and custom key profiles when detection fails
* Live FPS overlay powered by SurfaceFlinger TimeStats
* FPS pill controls for size, opacity, color, shape, corner radius, outline, and label
* Daily, every three days, or weekly update checks on a stable or prerelease channel
* Light, dark, and true black AMOLED themes

FPS monitor:
The overlay shows the frames an app actually presents, not the screen refresh rate. A 120 Hz display can still run a game at 30 FPS. Start monitoring from Settings or from the FPS tile. Drag the pill to move it and tap it for options.

Requirements:
* Android 11 or newer
* Shizuku installed, running, and authorized
* A display that supports more than one refresh rate
* Notification permission for background operation
* Overlay permission for the FPS monitor

akiHz is open source under the MIT license. It only changes display refresh rate settings.

Compatibility:
Tested on a Xiaomi 12T running Android 15 with HyperOS 2. Other manufacturers use best-effort support because each vendor exposes different settings keys. If detection fails, try Settings > Advanced > OEM override.
